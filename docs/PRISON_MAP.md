# Prison map

The main scene instances `scenes/PrisonMap.tscn`, generated by `scripts/prison_map.gd`.

## Layout
- **Central courtyard** — the hub, with benches and lamps. Existing player spawn and work stations remain aligned to this space.
- **Cell block** — two rows of simple bunk spaces, partitions, and a south-facing entrance.
- **Dining hall** — tables and benches, connected on the east side of the courtyard.
- **Kitchen** — attached beyond the dining hall.
- **Workshop** — work benches and shelving, connected west of the courtyard.
- **Laundry** — attached beyond the workshop.
- **Administration** — an office and notice board south of the courtyard.
- **Exercise yard** — an outdoor area with a garden bed and bench, connected through administration.

The map is a low-poly greybox built from primitive meshes with collision shapes. Room signage is included for navigation. Replace or decorate these placeholder structures with original art as the project grows.

## Editing
Room dimensions, wall positions, colors, and props are defined in `scripts/prison_map.gd`. The map is a fictional layout and does not reproduce a real facility.

## Testing status
The files have been committed to the repository, but the scene has not yet been imported or run in Godot and has not been tested on a Quest 3 headset.


## Interior detail pass
- Each cell has a hinged barred door, frame, bunk, mattress, pillow, locker, shelf, and simple washroom fixtures.
- The dining hall has a serving hatch and menu board; the kitchen has prep furniture, a sink, stove, and crates.
- The workshop has stools and a notice board; laundry has tubs and folded linen; administration has filing cabinets and chairs.
- The courtyard has a notice board and water fountain.
- Seven named NPCs are positioned in the courtyard, dining hall, kitchen, workshop, laundry, cell block, and exercise yard. They use the same dialogue interaction as the original character.

Cell doors use `scripts/cell_door.gd` and the normal interact input. NPC appearance and dialogue are configured in `scripts/npc.gd` and `scenes/Main.tscn`.

# Emergency scavenged food

The fictional prison survival loop includes two optional emergency food pickups:

- **Rat** — a small, non-graphic pickup placed in the cell-block area.
- **Cockroach** — a small, non-graphic pickup placed nearby.

Look at a pickup and use the normal interact control (E in the desktop prototype). It is collected once and adds one food portion to the existing food meter. Press F to consume a food portion.

These are fictional game items only; the mechanic is not advice about eating wild animals or insects in real life.

Implementation: `scripts/scavenged_food.gd`, instantiated in `scenes/Main.tscn`.
